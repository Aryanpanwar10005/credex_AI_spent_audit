# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: Credex AI Spend Audit

## 1. Value Proposition
*“Stop the AI Sprawl.”*
The Credex AI Spend Audit helps CFOs and CTOs reclaim unmanaged AI tool spend and redirect it toward higher-value infrastructure, specifically the Credex platform.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary Persona**: VP of Engineering / CTO at Series B+ startups.
- **Secondary Persona**: Finance Directors / CFOs looking for EBITDA improvements.
- **Pain Point**: Fragmented AI tool adoption leading to "Shadow AI" costs and redundant licenses.

## 3. Channels & Acquisition

### A. The "Shareable Audit" (Viral Loop)
- **Mechanism**: Every audit report is public and shareable.
- **Goal**: Encourage engineering managers to share the report with finance to "justify" the switch to Credex-backed infrastructure.

### B. Product-Led Growth (PLG)
- **Mechanism**: Free tool with high immediate value.
- **Conversion**: The "Save Results" form captures high-intent leads for the Credex enterprise sales team.

### C. Content Marketing
- **Mechanism**: Use the `market_research` and `user_interviews` data to create "The State of AI Spend 2026" whitepaper.
- **SEO**: Targeting long-tail keywords like "Cursor vs Copilot enterprise cost" and "AI tool redundancy audit".

## 4. The Credex Up-Sell Path
1. **Audit Phase**: Find $10k+ in annual AI tool savings.
2. **Recommendation Phase**: Suggest "Credex Infrastructure Credits" ($1k-$10k) to offset the cost of switching.
3. **Conversion Phase**: Lead submits email to claim credits.
4. **Sales Phase**: High-touch outreach from Credex to transition the team to the full Credex ecosystem.

## 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **Viral Coefficient**: Average number of times a report is shared.
- **Lead Quality**: % of audits with >$5k in identified annual savings.
- **Brand Sentiment**: Perceived authority of Credex in the "AI Governance" space.
